Your payment went through. Polar, our payment processor, delivers your license key with the order — open the 'Access purchase' link in your receipt email to find it (check your spam folder too).

What happens next

  1. Open the 'Access purchase' link in your Polar receipt — your license key is listed under the order's benefits (a separate key email may also arrive).
  2. Install FoldFX with the signed download below — Android 8.0 or newer.
  3. Open FoldFX, go to License, paste your key — done. Your key covers one device.

One key, one device: to move FoldFX to another phone, use License → Deactivate this device first, then activate on the new one.

Important: the license key is not the invoice number on your receipt. If the app says the key was not accepted, you are likely pasting the invoice number — copy the license key from 'Access purchase' instead.

One honest note: FoldFX is still in beta and might have issues on some devices — hinge behavior, the live wallpaper and the experimental overlay can vary across foldable models and Android versions. If anything misbehaves, email FoldFX@protonmail.com — fixes ship as free updates.
